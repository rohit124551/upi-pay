UPIPay v2.0

Zero-backend bulk UPI payout terminal & dynamic QR generator

1 What is UPI Pay and how does it work?

UPI Pay is an open-source, client-side web suite for making multiple UPI payments safely. It cleans pasted lists or uploaded spreadsheets, merges duplicate entries for the same UPI ID, balances your totals, and generates individual QR codes for each recipient so you can pay them one-by-one from your mobile banking app without typing errors.

2 Does UPI Pay ever ask for my UPI PIN or bank password?

Never. UPI Pay never asks for, receives, or has access to your UPI PIN, MPIN, OTP, or bank login. Your UPI PIN is entered solely on your own phone inside your official, certified UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, etc.).

3 Does UPI Pay touch, hold, or route my money?

No. UPI Pay is not a payment intermediary, wallet, or payment aggregator. It only creates standard NPCI-compliant payment intent deep links (upi://pay). The actual money transfer happens directly from your bank account to the recipient's bank account via the NPCI payment network.

4 How does duplicate detection protect me?

If the same recipient appears multiple times across your list (for example, ₹400 for lunch and ₹1,200 for supplies), UPI Pay automatically merges them into a single payment of ₹1,600, preserving audit history so you don't accidentally pay them twice.

5 Are shareable payment links (/pay/) public or crawled by search engines?

No. Payment details are encoded entirely in the URL parameters. No link data is stored on any server. Furthermore, search engine crawlers are strictly forbidden from indexing payment links via robots.txt Disallow and noindex, nofollow robot directives.

6 What are the official NPCI transaction limits & frequency caps?

The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) establish standard transaction and velocity limits across the UPI network:

Standard UPI (P2P Transfers):
  • 20 transactions/day count cap per bank account (rolling 24h).
  • ₹1,00,000/day standard peer-to-peer monetary limit.
  • ₹5,00,000 max for approved taxes, hospitals, colleges & IPOs.
  • ₹5,000 cap for first 24h on new setup or PIN reset.
UPI Lite (Small-Value Payments):
  • Up to ₹1,000 per transaction (PIN-less instant pay).
  • Up to ₹5,000 maximum wallet balance at any point.
  • Up to ₹10,000 cumulative daily spend/top-up limit.

7 Can I process batches with more than 20 people using UPI Pay?

Yes! UPI Pay allows you to paste, clean, and reconcile batches of any size (e.g. 50 or 100 payees). If you encounter your bank's 20-transaction daily velocity limit, you can complete the first 20 transactions today, and complete the remaining payments the next day or from a secondary linked bank account. You can export an updated CSV run log at any stage.

8 What should I do if a payment fails or is declined?

Press E on your keyboard or tap Mark Error. You can enter the failure reason (such as "Bank server down" or "Daily limit reached"). The item will be tagged with a red warning badge, and you can click Copy Errors to isolate and retry only the failed recipients later.

Why UPI Pay Exists

Personal and retail bank accounts in India do not have access to bulk-payout APIs. Small businesses, freelance collectives, event organizers, and student teams are often forced to manually re-type 20 to 50 UPI IDs and amounts on a mobile keyboard—a tedious process where a single misplaced digit can send money to an unintended stranger.

UPI Pay solves this with an open-source, keyboard-driven, verified workflow that guarantees totals reconcile before a single rupee is sent.

Privacy by Architecture

UPI Pay is architected with zero backend servers. We cannot collect, store, view, or sell your financial data because it never leaves your browser sandbox.

1. Zero Data Collection & Storage

We collect no personal data, payment lists, recipient UPI IDs, phone numbers, or financial amounts. No analytics, tracking pixels, or cross-site telemetry are included.

2. Local Browser Storage Use

Your browser's localStorage is utilized solely for your convenience: saving theme preference (Light/Dark) and optional high-value warning limits.

3. User Responsibility & Safety Warning

UPI transactions are instantaneous and irreversible. You are solely responsible for verifying the payee name and bank handle displayed on your phone's banking screen before entering your UPI PIN. We strongly recommend sending a ₹1 test payment to new payees first.
