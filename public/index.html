UPI Pay — Bulk UPI Payouts and Dynamic QR Terminal

A 100% client-side web application for retail bank accounts to execute bulk UPI payouts and generate dynamic NPCI QR codes without corporate APIs or gateway fees.

UPI Pay Terminal & Bulk Payouts

UPI Pay solves bulk disbursement challenges for Indian small businesses, organizers, and creators. Instead of paying exorbitant payment gateway integration costs, recurring fees, or going through corporate KYC verification, you can disburse payments directly from your existing savings or current bank accounts using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or Navi.

Messy List Ingestion from WhatsApp and Spreadsheets

Easily paste unformatted lists of names, phone numbers, and amounts copied from WhatsApp messages, Telegram groups, accounting sheets, or CSV files. The built-in intelligent parser extracts valid Virtual Payment Addresses (VPAs) and currency amounts, sanitizing formatting noise instantly.

Smart 4-Way Payout Reconciliation

Prevent overpaying and eliminate financial errors before scanning. UPI Pay automatically validates calculated sums against your declared master invoice total, flags duplicate UPI IDs within the batch, identifies malformed addresses, and offers row-by-row confirmation workflows.

Dynamic NPCI UPI QR Generator

Generate instant, standardized NPCI-compliant dynamic UPI QR codes. Each QR code is pre-filled with the exact payee Virtual Payment Address, amount, and reference note, enabling quick payment scanning without manual entry errors.

100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

Zero backend servers, zero database storage, zero tracking cookies. All regex parsing, data sanitation, batch state persistence, and QR code rendering execute exclusively inside your web browser. Your sensitive financial data never leaves your device.
