Privacy Policy & Data Security

Last Updated: November 2026. Complete transparency on how UPI Pay handles your data: zero remote servers, zero databases, and zero tracking.

Launch UPI Pay Terminal

Zero Backend Data Collection

UPI Pay is intentionally designed as an offline-first, client-side application. When you use UPI Pay to paste messy lists, process bulk payouts, reconcile amounts, or generate dynamic QR codes:

  • No Server Transmission: Beneficiary names, UPI Virtual Payment Addresses (VPAs), payment amounts, transaction remarks, and contact numbers never leave your browser.
  • No Backend Databases: There is no central server, backend API, or cloud database connected to UPI Pay that could store, view, or leak your financial details.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, Hotjar, or any commercial user tracking scripts.

Local Storage & Session Persistence

UPI Pay uses your browser's standard localStorage API purely for convenience features, such as remembering your UI preferences (dark/light theme) and maintaining your current payout batch draft in case you refresh the page. This data remains strictly on your local device and is never synchronized to any remote cloud service. You can clear this data at any time by clicking "Clear Batch" or clearing your browser cache.

NPCI Protocol & Bank Interoperability

UPI Pay generates standardized NPCI UPI URLs following the official specification: upi://pay?pa=...&am=...&tn=.... When you scan a QR code using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or your mobile banking app, the transaction is executed directly between your banking app and the recipient's bank over NPCI's secure interbank network. UPI Pay never acts as an intermediary or financial custodian.
